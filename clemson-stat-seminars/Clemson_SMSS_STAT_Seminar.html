Non-Additive Models for Multi-Fidelity Simulations

Dr. Junoh Heo
Department of Statistical Sciences, Wake Forest University
🕚 11:15 AM–12:05 PM 📍 Long Hall 207

Computer simulations have become indispensable tools for analyzing and optimizing complex systems across scientific and engineering disciplines. This talk develops flexible statistical models, scalable software implementations, and sequential design strategies for complex multi-fidelity emulation and optimization.

Bio

Junoh Heo is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Statistical Sciences at Wake Forest University. He received his Ph.D. in Statistics from Michigan State University in 2026, advised by Chih-Li Sung. His research focuses on computer experiments, multi-fidelity simulation, uncertainty quantification, and the development of scalable statistical methods for complex computer models. His work has applications across the physical, biological, and engineering sciences.
